  [ ] You can really deliver an invitation to a customer.
  [ ] The Burmese was checked by a Burmese reader.
  [ ] Pictures show only fictional couples.

Boost settings (set them yourself, then check the total before paying)
  Goal       Get more messages (Messenger or WhatsApp)
  Location   Myanmar (or Yangon / Mandalay / Naypyidaw)
  Age        22 to 40
  Language   Burmese
  Interests  Weddings, bridal, engagement (keep broad at first)
  Budget     Start small for 5 to 7 days. Raise it only if you get messages.
  Payment    Your own method. Review the total, then press Boost.

After 1 to 2 days (Insights or Ads Manager)
  - Look at messages received and cost per message.
  - If cost is high, change the picture first, then the headline.
